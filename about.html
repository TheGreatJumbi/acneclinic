Specialist-Led Care

Expert dermatology care from a board-certified specialist

The Acne Clinic is run by Dr. Aaron Secrest, MD, PhD, MPH, a board-certified dermatologist and Clinical Director of Dermatology for Te Waipounamu (South Island public dermatology service).

Dr. Secrest sees acne patients every Tuesday in Christchurch through Dermatology Doctors.

Acne is common, treatable, and nothing to feel embarrassed about. Whether you're dealing with a few stubborn breakouts or acne that's affecting your confidence, you'll get a clear plan and treatment that actually works.
