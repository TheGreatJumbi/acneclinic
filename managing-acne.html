Gentle Cleansing

Use a mild, non-comedogenic cleanser twice daily. Avoid harsh scrubbing which can worsen inflammation.

Don't Pick

Picking or squeezing acne can lead to scarring and prolonged healing. Let treatments do their work.

Consistent Routine

Acne treatments take time. Stick with your routine for at least 6-8 weeks before expecting results.

Sun Protection

Some acne medications increase sun sensitivity. Use a non-comedogenic sunscreen daily.
